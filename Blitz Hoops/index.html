How to play

Hot Dogs shoot right, Turbo Toads shoot left. Green releases have the best odds, followed by amber and red. The shot meter shows your range tier. Longer shots need a later release, have a smaller green window, and are less likely to go in. Nearby defenders lower the odds. Tap Shoot near the rim to dunk. Defenders in your path or at the rim make dunks less reliable. Made dunks get an instant replay. Shoot from outside the arc for three. On defense, jump near the shooter to block or swipe up close to steal. Chase loose balls for rebounds. The shot clock gives you 12 seconds on a new possession. Release the ball before zero or the other team takes over. Tied games go to a full 28-second overtime. Play another overtime if the score is still tied.

Swiping from behind can draw a reach-in foul. Contact during a shot can send the shooter to the free-throw line. At the line, tap Free throw when the fast-moving marker hits the narrow green zone. Waiting too long counts as a miss. The halftime show replays first-half highlights. Tap Skip halftime on your phone or use the shared screen.

The gold bar at your feet shows how much stamina you have left. Time on court wears you down, and Turbo tires you out faster. Hard contact can shake up a tired player. The referee stops both clocks for a replay and resumes play with the same possession. An injured phone player can shorten the five-second slowdown. Read the recovery instructions on your phone and tap Ready. Memorize the order on the shared screen, then repeat it with Ice, Tape, and Stretch on your phone. Halftime restores some stamina and clears injuries.

Everyone gets their own practice ball in the join lobby. Warm-up shots use the match’s timing and open-shot odds. Basic goes straight to tip-off with CPU teammates. In Chaos, the lowest-numbered player on each team drafts CPUs to fill open slots. Each human and CPU gets a different ability. Tap its named button on your phone during play. Powers recharge after 18 seconds of live play. The first joined player can change mode on their phone before starting. Chaos captains can read CPU ratings and abilities on their phone before confirming a draft pick. Shots released before the quarter horn can still score.

Up to three phone players can join each team. Your saved nickname appears above your player and your accent color marks your jersey stripe. Team jerseys stay coral or teal so you can recognize each side. The CPU fills empty spots. Start without phones to watch a CPU exhibition.
