# Ringout card guide

Ringout is a local arena brawler. Tap shove for a quick close-range hit, hold and release for a stronger launch, dash to reposition, and time a dodge to parry. Upgrades change how your shove works, improve its setup, or reward landing a hit. In Rounds everyone begins cardless. After each decisive fight, the losers pick upgrades simultaneously. The lowest score gets an extra choice. First to five with the lead wins by default.

## Rounds deck (18 cards)

- **Pinball God** — Fighters you hit with a charged shove rebound off one wall.
- **Triple Trouble** — Your shove covers three lanes. Side lanes trade force for coverage.
- **Echo Fist** — Charged shoves repeat in place after a short beat at reduced force.
- **Rocket Boots** — Dash primes your next shove to travel farther. Land it to recover part of your dash.
- **Gravity Hands** — Charging draws nearby fighters closer. Release to shove them back out.
- **Mirror Match** — Perfect parries prime your next shove to hit behind you too.
- **Swap Meet** — A full shove swaps you with a fighter when both landings are clear.
- **Domino Effect** — Fighters you launch kick a pulse sideways when they hit a wall.
- **Boomerang** — A charged shove sends a delayed return shove back toward you from its outer edge.
- **Slingshot** — Dash against a launch to bank its momentum for your next shove.
- **Puppet Master** — Dash leaves a decoy. Charged shoves also come from that spot at reduced force.
- **Black Hole** — A charged shove leaves a short pull field at the edge of its reach for your next setup.
- **Punching Bag** — Move slower and absorb part of a hit to power your next shove.
- **Chain Lightning** — A shove that lands sends a pulse toward another nearby fighter.
- **House of Cards** — Your shove hits build bumpers. Fighters and pulses can use them.
- **Last Laugh** — Once per fight, brake a dangerous launch near the rim and prime a stronger shove.
- **Reverse Card** — Perfect parries prime your next shove to pull fighters toward you.
- **Superball** — Charged shoves launch fighters into two elastic wall rebounds.

## Trick Draft deck (30 cards)

### Starting Tricks

- **Pitcher** — Throw a rubber ball anyone can bat. It banks off walls.
- **Grappler** — Hook a wall, loose prop, or sliding fighter. Dash to pull sideways.
- **Puppeteer** — Deploy a puppet or switch which body you steer. Protect your real fighter.
- **Rigger** — Place a directional bumper. Anyone can aim it with a shove.
- **Daredevil** — Enter a steerable slide. Shove while sliding to spend speed.
- **Bellringer** — Place a bell. Shove it to ring along your aim.

### Foundations

- **Wide Sweep** — Cover a wide arc and operate several props. Each hit is weaker.
- **Needlepoint** — A narrow shove reaches farther and hits harder.
- **Quick Draw** — Reach partial charge sooner. Maximum force stays the same.
- **Heavy Hands** — Full shoves hit harder. A full miss takes longer to recover.
- **Low Center** — Resist shoves at the cost of slower acceleration.
- **Cornering** — Turn more sharply while riding a slide or steering a puppet.
- **Long Reach** — Extend your Trick’s working range.
- **Quick Feet** — Recover dash sooner when it misses every fighter.
- **Recoil Drive** — Charged shoves kick you backward.
- **Spot Weld** — Tap a movable prop to hold it in place briefly.

### Twists

- **Wrong Number** — Parry to return a weaker traveling shove along your aim.
- **Carom** — Launches you cause bank off one wall and can pass their momentum on.
- **Stunt Double** — Charged shoves also fire from your setup. Wall taps supply a cardboard stand-in.
- **Velcro** — Tap to carry a loose prop. Dash to fling it.
- **Tripwire** — Dash to string a wire from your setup. Dodge to cross it safely.
- **Friction Tax** — A charged shove makes the contact point slick for everyone.
- **Second Verse** — Tap a fighter or your own setup to load one full-circle shove.
- **Hot Potato** — Charge to stick a fuse on a fighter or prop. Tap to pass it before it bursts.

### Breakers

- **Pinball Cabinet** — Your first bank or bumper kick in a rally splits off a second shove.
- **Slingshot** — Release a stretched cable to fling the far end. Dashing alongside motion can supply a cable.
- **Body Double** — Once per fight, press Trick during your decoy’s fall warning to swap if the landing is safe.
- **Pass the Buck** — Parry to deploy your Trick at your next charged shove’s endpoint.
- **No Brakes** — Dash out of a fast slide to send its momentum down the old path.
- **Dead Man’s Switch** — Queue a weaker replay at a charged shove’s target prop or endpoint.
