How to play

Stay on the platform and knock opponents off. Hold and release Push to charge it, even while dashing. Hold past the red full-charge timer to launch yourself backward. Ferry riders are protected while off the dock. Double-tap Dash, hold to aim, and keep holding until Super Dash launches. Release early to cancel. Dodge just before a push to parry it.

Modes

Versus First to two round wins.
Rounds Everyone starts with the same moves. Hold and release to launch nearby fighters. Upgrades change the shove’s reach, shape, or what happens when it lands. After a fight, everyone except the winner picks an upgrade at the same time. First to five with the outright lead wins. Trick Draft and Classic Rounds keep their original card pools.
Endless co-op Fight CPU waves and draft every two clears.

During drafts, check your build and look for offers marked Combos with. Aim with the stick or movement keys. Tap push to bat arrows or move mines. A colored ring or countdown marks an incoming effect. A ghost with Last Word can aim and tap push once after the windup.

Player setup

Use the stick or D-pad to browse options, A / × to select, Up to reach the tabs, or B / ○ for the next page, and Start to ready. Keyboard players use R or / to ready, or click their Ready button.
