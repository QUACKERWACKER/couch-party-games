RINGOUT

Start.command opens the shared launcher using the local phone relay on
port 3000. Start that relay as described in the project’s Joinstick.md,
or use the hosted games launcher.

Open the shared launcher, start a Phone controllers room, then scan its
QR code or enter the room code on your phone. Select Ringout on the launcher.
Tap Join, customize on the shared screen, and tap Ready. Up to six phones
can play. The saved phone profile supplies nickname and fighter outline.

A is Dash, B is Parry, X is Push, and Y is Emote or the equipped Trick.
Use the left stick to move and the middle stick to aim. Hold X to charge,
then release. Pause and Resume stay on the center button. During an upgrade
break, read and pick a card on your phone, then watch the arena again.

For local play, keep the launcher and phone relay running. Character looks,
menu themes, and co-op records are saved in cookies in your browser.
Phone vibration depends on browser support. All phones disconnecting pauses combat.

Rounds starts everyone cardless. Tap shove up close, hold and release for
a stronger shove, dash to reposition, and time a dodge to parry.
Upgrades change the shove itself or reward landing one.
After a decisive fight, the losing players draft simultaneously. The
lowest score gets an extra choice. First to five wins by default.
Trick Draft, Rounds Quick, and Classic Rounds offer the earlier rules.
Open the ? menu for phone controls.

Optional regression checks (Node.js 18+): node tests/cards.test.js
